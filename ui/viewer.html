Reference: delivery options, actions and events

Delivery options (what a dealer offers)

same_dayDue 20:00 on the day the car is ready. Only when the request arrives by 15:00 and the drive can end 2 h before the deadline.
next_dayDue 20:00 on the next business day (Friday → Monday).
within_3_daysDue 20:00 on the 3rd business day after ready, no later than the last Monday.
private deliveryBooked hour; due 30 min after it ends. Trade-ins and private deliveries carry one option only.

Late refunds

≤ 2 h10% back
2–24 h25% back
> 24 h, ≤ 2 business days50% back
> 2 business days100% back
not deliveredno price + penalty 2× price

Actions (what our system can do)

quoteA price per option, before respondBy (15 min). Decline = quote far above list.
postPut a gig (stops, audience, fee schedule of 1–48 rising steps) in the drivers' app.
offerDirect offer to one driver for 10 min. Max 3/driver/day, 3 open per gig.
repriceReplace the fee schedule of an unbooked gig.
withdrawTake an unbooked gig out of the app.
releaseEnd a booking before the driver leaves (25% / 50% / 100% fee; free after driver_late).

Events (what can happen)

price_requestDealer asks for a price.
order_won / order_lostDealer's decision at respondBy.
gig_viewed / gig_bookedDriver opened / took a gig.
gig_dropped / no_showDriver gave it back; gig is open again.
gig_expiredNo booking before a stop window ended.
driver_lateDriver will pick up late (can release free).
deadline_closeNo driver 3 h before lastPickupAt.
visit_failednot_ready / closed / licence / no_plates.
order_changed / cancelledNew place or deadline / order gone.
forecastSnow coming (level 5+).
ledgerA line of money in or out.
